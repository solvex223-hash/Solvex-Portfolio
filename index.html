Concept / proposal · Website & direct booking system

J's Signature Hotel

A complete digital experience concept created around the needs of a modern hotel. This is a SolveX concept and proposal, not a completed client project.

The problem: Hotels can lose opportunities when guests depend entirely on third-party booking channels or fragmented enquiry processes.

The concept: Rebuild the hotel's website into a direct digital booking experience where guests can explore rooms, view information, check availability, make reservations and communicate directly with the hotel.

Potential features: Rooms & suites · Room details · Availability · Direct booking · Guest information · Events and conference spaces · Restaurant and facilities · Gallery · WhatsApp communication · Booking management

The goal isn't simply a better-looking hotel website. It's a stronger digital booking channel.
